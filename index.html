Why this tool is different

📄

ATS Optimization

More than 75% of resumes are filtered by Applicant Tracking Systems before any human reads them. We boost your keyword coverage automatically.

🛡️

Hallucination Control

Other AI tools invent skills you do not have. Our prompt engineering uses strict anti-hallucination rules — the AI uses only the facts from your real resume.

🔬

Four Strategies

Compare four prompt engineering strategies side by side: Baseline, Naive AI, ATS-Optimized, and ATS + Anti-Hallucination. See the trade-offs in real numbers.

🔒

Privacy First

Everything runs in your browser. Your resume never goes to our server — there is no server. Only your browser and the Grok API.

How it works

  1. Upload your resume (PDF or DOCX).
  2. Paste the job description you are applying to.
  3. Choose a prompt strategy (or compare all four).
  4. Receive a tailored resume with full ATS and hallucination report.

About this research

This web application is part of a Bachelor thesis at Riga Nordic University, programme 42484 Information Systems. The work compares four prompt engineering strategies and measures their effect on ATS keyword coverage, hallucination rate, tailoring score, and readability.
